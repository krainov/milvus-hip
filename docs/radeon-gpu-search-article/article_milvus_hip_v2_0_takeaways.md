# Bringing Milvus GPU search to Radeon: a port, a benchmark, and what we learned

**Key takeaways.** We ported Milvus GPU approximate nearest-neighbour (ANN) search from CUDA to HIP and ran it end to end on a Radeon RX 7900 XTX. On the same GIST-1M recipes, recall@10 matches the NVIDIA build (0.9910, 0.9918, and 0.9909). Uncompressed `GPU_IVF_FLAT` is faster on the 7900 XTX than on an RTX 4080 at every nprobe we ran — about **+15–30%** queries per second at nprobe 8 (nprobe is how many index clusters a query scans). At nprobe 128 those estimates agree near **1.3×**. Compressed `GPU_IVF_PQ` QPS is bimodal, two modes about 1.7× apart. A median can sit in the fast mode on the 7900 XTX and in the slow mode on the RTX 4080, which is how the harness median can read **+36%** at nprobe 8 and **−22%** at nprobe 128. Mode against the same mode, the 7900 XTX is about **0.87×** the 4080 at low nprobe and about **0.65×** at high nprobe. Those curves do not cross.
